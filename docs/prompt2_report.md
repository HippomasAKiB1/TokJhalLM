## 7. Concerns and risks

1. **Real romanized Banglish is about 23M tokens, not 29M.** About 2.9M of the 14.6M "target-script" words are English fragments inside Bengali-script pages. The only sizable real Banglish sources are BanglishRev (~7M Latin words, mixed with English reviews) and FineWeb `ben_Latn` (4.4M). Transliteration can't fix register: synthetic romanized Wikipedia will read formal and won't code-mix English the way BnSentMix does.
2. **BnSentMix and BanglishRev likely share sources.** There are 34 hard train hits and 61 soft ones from BanglishRev alone. It isn't test leakage, but it means pretraining on BanglishRev partially pretrains on the BnSentMix distribution, which helps us and could flatter the comparison. Baselines (BanglishBERT) don't get that benefit, so the report must disclose it.
3. **BnSentMix label quality has a ceiling.** 19 duplicate groups carried conflicting labels (38 rows dropped), "neutral" is mostly questions, and "mixed" is only 8.9% (~175 dev examples). Macro-F1 will swing on the mixed class, so per-class F1 and bootstrap CIs are essential.
